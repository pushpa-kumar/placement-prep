      How to read this

      Reported Real = a source explicitly states this was actually asked to a real candidate in a real interview or OA. Practice = a plausible/likely question found online with no first-hand confirmation (prep-site material, or a self-labeled anecdotal/AI-assisted repo). Self-Generated = written for this question bank rather than sourced from anywhere — useful practice, but never claimed to have been actually asked.

      Sources include LeetCode Discuss, GitHub interview-experience repositories, Blind, Glassdoor (via search snippets/archives), Wall Street Oasis, GeeksforGeeks, and personal engineering blogs. Full source and link are shown on every sourced entry.

      Reddit, Glassdoor, 1point3acres, and Blind were heavily bot/CAPTCHA-blocked during research, so verbatim coverage from those sites is thinner than their real share of this content — treat gaps per company as research limits, not as an absence of real questions.
